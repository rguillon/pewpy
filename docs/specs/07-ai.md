# 07 — AI player

The game shall contain an AI that learns to play by itself: one neural network (the "brain") flying every regular
ship (it knows which one it flies; never the Tester), to watch in the game and to measure how hard each level is for
each ship. *(placeholder: every number; not tuned on long runs yet)*

## Modes

- **AI-1** **Learning** (without a window): the brain shall learn on every level with every ship, with the curriculum
  (AI-22), for a given number of generations (100 by default), printing each generation, its best try's fitness, the
  worlds it trains on and, at each check, for each ship the share of the levels it clears (with one life) and how far
  into them it gets on average. Learning shall go on from the saved brain next time.
- **AI-2** **Learning on chosen levels**: no curriculum; the brain shall train on the levels given only (for example
  1-1 alone, or every level drawn at random), and each check shall play them 48 times in all with each ship (each level
  as often). An option shall start a new brain instead of the saved one.
- **AI-3** **Win rates** (without a window): the saved brain (a new one if there is none) shall play every level with
  each ship, 10 times with different randomness and one life each (both settable); it shall print, level after level,
  each ship's share of runs that clear the level, then the same for each world and for every level. Nothing is saved.
- **AI-4** **AI playing** (the Dev menu, UIA-33): the saved brain (a new one if there is none) shall play from the level
  picked with the ship picked, by the game's rules (all its lives, a death restarts the level), going on to the next
  level with its score, lives and weapons once one is cleared; at game over, a new game from the level picked, the
  brain loaded again (it may have learned since). Nothing is learned or saved.
- **AI-5** Learning and the win rates shall run in worker processes (all the cores but one, unless given).

## Files

- **AI-6** The brain shall be saved in the game's data and ship with the game, with its generation, the worlds open, and
  its training's history (every 10 generations: the best and mean fitness, the worlds open, and for each ship the share
  of levels cleared and how far into them it got). Learning may save it elsewhere instead.
- **AI-7** A brain made for other inputs or outputs shall be ignored: learning starts again.

## What the AI sees

- **AI-8** A danger radar planning ahead: for each of 9 moves (8 directions and staying put), the ship flies it for
  0.25 s, then the best of the 9 moves until 0.8 s (with the ship's inertia; the enemy shots and enemies within 0.9 wu
  going on at their speed, a snaking shot as wide as its snaking). For each move: how long before a hit (closer than
  0.02 wu; 1 for none) and how much room is left on the way (from 0 to 1, 0.2 wu or more being 1).
- **AI-9** From the radar: the safest move's direction, and the **move to aim**: among the safe moves (scoring within
  0.1 of the safest), the one bringing the ship nearest its target: onto the nearest pickup (where it will be in
  0.25 s; when the pickup is above the middle of the screen, under it, waiting for it to drift down), else under the
  boss (under its part nearest across while its core is armored), else under the nearest enemy above it on screen;
  near the middle of the screen's height (y = 0), minded 0.3 times as much as being under the target.
- **AI-10** The 6 nearest enemy shots: where they are from the ship and how fast they go.
- **AI-11** 8 lanes across the screen: how many enemy shots and enemies in each, above the ship.
- **AI-12** The ship: where it is, its speed, its health, whether it is invulnerable, its kind (its top speed and size,
  and one input per ship, 1 for the one it flies), the weapon selected and the three weapons' levels.
- **AI-13** The 3 nearest enemies and the nearest pickup within 1 wu (where they are), and the boss (where it is, its
  health).
- **AI-14** Its repairs: whether any enemy on screen can be hurt (not an armored core nor an arriving boss), how long
  since it last fired (as a share of the 1.5 s before repairs start, 1 once they have) and how fast its ship repairs
  (0.5 health per second as 1).

## What it does

- **AI-15** The brain shall be a small neural network: one hidden layer of 24 neurons (tanh) and a direct path from the
  inputs to the outputs; 3 outputs: the stick (x and y, from −1 to 1) and the Fire button (pressed above 0).
- **AI-16** The weapon shall be chosen by a rule, not learned: the weapon with the highest level, bullets then laser
  then missiles when tied *(placeholder)*.
- **AI-17** It shall decide 30 times a second and hold the stick in between, like a player's reaction time.
- **AI-18** A new brain shall start with small random weights (its hidden layer's effect on the outputs 0.03 times as
  big) and the direct path closed, except: from the radar's move to aim to the stick (3 times over, the stick clamped)
  and from "an enemy can be hurt" to the Fire button (held then, released otherwise: firing at nothing only puts the
  repairs off). So before learning anything it dodges, aims, picks up pickups and repairs when there is nothing to
  shoot.

## How it learns

- **AI-19** Evolution strategies: each generation shall try the brain's weights with 32 random nudges and their
  opposites (64 tries), each try playing the same runs, one per ship, each on a level drawn among the open worlds'
  levels (with the same randomness), with one life. The brain moves towards the nudges that did better (ranked: only
  the order counts), with Adam steps (nudges of 0.02, steps of 0.01) and a little weight decay (0.005).
- **AI-20** A try's fitness shall be its runs' average, each run counting: 1 per second it got on the level's clock
  (which waits while a boss is fought: dodging a boss without destroying it earns nothing), 1 per 250 points scored, 5
  per pickup taken, up to 60 per boss for the share of it destroyed (the mini boss as the final one), and 150 for
  clearing the level plus up to 30 for the health left. A run gives up 60 s per boss after the final boss is due.
  Training runs play without the ship's arrival and departure.
- **AI-21** Every 10 generations the brain shall play every level (of the levels given) with each ship, with one life;
  the share it clears and how far it gets on average (reaching the final boss counts half way; destroying it, all the
  way) are its progress; it is saved then.
- **AI-22** Curriculum: a new brain shall train on world 1 only; at each check, once it gets half way into the open
  worlds' levels on average over every ship, the next world opens.
